Court decisions

Case What it decided Source
Katiba Institute & another v Attorney General & another, Constitutional Petition 209 of 2016, High Court, 14 July 2017 Declared parts of the recall rules for MPs (Elections Act ss.45, 46, 48) and MCAs (County Governments Act ss.27, 28) unconstitutional Kenya Law
The 2017 presidential election petition, Supreme Court, 1 September 2017 Declared the presidential election invalid, 4 to 2; a fresh election was held on 26 October 2017 and upheld on 20 November 2017 The EastAfrican; Jurist, 20 Nov 2017
Willie Kimani murder trial, High Court, verdict 22 July 2022, sentences 3 February 2023 Three police officers and an informer convicted; one officer acquitted Al Jazeera, 3 Feb 2023; Nation timeline
Nyandarua traders’ petition, High Court, May 2024 Status quo order on licence and permit fees while the case was heard. Final outcome yet to be confirmed The Standard, 30 May 2024
MP Onesmus Gikaria’s challenge to an IEBC fine, High Court, 8 October 2026 (as reported; we did not read the judgment) Dismissed the MP’s challenge to an IEBC fine of KES 2.5 million over remarks implying voter inducement The Standard; Capital FM

Where the stories come from

The course is built from the author’s own YouTube explainer series on the Constitution (23 scripts, October 2026), especially Video 01 (What is a right?), Video 03 (The election the court cancelled), Video 06 (The lawyer killed for doing his job), Video 17 (The smallest ballot, the biggest bite) and Video 18 (The KES 500 vote), and from the author’s earlier written guide Kenya’s 2010 Constitution in plain words (Parts 1 and 2). Otieno and Akinyi are made-up characters from that series. Every fact taken from those materials was re-checked against the sources above.

What we could not confirm

  • An SMS code for checking voter registration: we found none on IEBC’s website, so the course leaves it out.
  • The police emergency number: the National Police Service website didn’t load (a certificate error), so we left the number out.
  • Voter registration dates for 2027: the IEBC pages we read don’t give them. Check iebc.or.ke.
  • Whether any MP or MCA recall has ever been completed: we found none, so it stays yet to be confirmed.
  • Whether Parliament replaced the MP recall rules struck down in 2017: we couldn’t find that it did. Ask IEBC.
  • The final outcome of the Nyandarua fees case: we haven’t found the final ruling.
