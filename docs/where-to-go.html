Where to go: who to call, and for what

Every number and address on this page was copied from that office’s own website on Friday, 27 November 2026. Numbers change. Before you rely on one, open the website next to it and check.

Rule for this course: a phone number appears in a lesson only if it is on this page first. If you find a number that has changed, please open an issue or a pull request with the link to the office’s own page.

The offices, one by one

IEBC: Independent Electoral and Boundaries Commission

  • What it does: registers voters, keeps the voters’ roll, runs elections, registers candidates, enforces the electoral code of conduct, and does voter education (Constitution, Art 88(4)).
  • Website: iebc.or.ke
  • Check your registration: verify.iebc.or.ke. You type your ID or passport number and your year of birth.
  • Phone: (254) 020 2877000
  • Email: info@iebc.or.ke
  • Head office: Anniversary Towers, 6th Floor, University Way, Nairobi. P.O. Box 45371-00100.
  • Your constituency office: IEBC has an office in every constituency. The list is on its website: Physical locations of constituency offices (PDF).
  • Complaint forms: iebc.or.ke/resources/?Complaint_Forms
  • Note: we did not find an SMS number for checking your registration on IEBC’s own website. Some people remember one from past elections. Check iebc.or.ke before using any SMS code.

NCIC: National Cohesion and Integration Commission

  • What it does: fights hate speech and ethnic hatred, and works for peace between communities. It handles complaints under the National Cohesion and Integration Act, 2008.
  • Website: cohesion.go.ke
  • Toll free: 1547; SMS: 1547
  • Email: info@cohesion.go.ke
  • Report online: cohesion.go.ke/report

EACC: Ethics and Anti-Corruption Commission

  • What it does: investigates corruption and enforces Chapter Six (leadership and integrity) of the Constitution (Art 79).
  • Website: eacc.go.ke
  • Toll free: 1551
  • Phone: (020) 4997000; Mobile 0709 781 000
  • Email: eacc@integrity.go.ke; Reports: report@integrity.go.ke
  • Report online: eacc.go.ke/en/default/report-corruption/
  • Head office: Integrity Centre, Jakaya Kikwete / Valley Road junction, Nairobi. P.O. Box 61130-00200.

IPOA: Independent Policing Oversight Authority

  • What it does: receives and investigates complaints against police officers: deaths and injuries caused by police, beatings, unlawful arrests, bribes.
  • Website: ipoa.go.ke
  • Toll free: 1559 (the website says it runs 24 hours a day)
  • Phone: (020) 490 6000
  • Email: complaints@ipoa.go.ke; info@ipoa.go.ke
  • Post: P.O. Box 23035-00100, Nairobi

KNCHR: Kenya National Commission on Human Rights

  • What it does: receives and investigates complaints that a right in the Bill of Rights has been denied, violated or threatened. Every person has the right to complain to it (Art 59(3)).
  • Website: knchr.org
  • SMS: 22359
  • Phone: +254 020 3969000; Mobile 0726 610 159; WhatsApp 0798 849 871
  • Email: complaint@knchr.org (complaints); haki@knchr.org (general)
  • Report online: knchr.org/Report-Violation
  • Head office: CVS Plaza, 1st Floor, Kasuku Lane, off Lenana Road, Nairobi. P.O. Box 74359-00200.

CAJ: Commission on Administrative Justice (the Ombudsman)

  • What it does: handles complaints against government offices and officers: delays, rudeness, unfair treatment, abuse of power, and refusal to give you information you have a right to.
  • Website: ombudsman.go.ke
  • Toll free: 0800 221 349
  • Phone: 254-20-2270000
  • Email: info@ombudsman.go.ke
  • Complain online: cmis.ombudsman.go.ke
  • Head office: West End Towers, 2nd Floor, off Waiyaki Way, Westlands, Nairobi. P.O. Box 20414-00200.

NGEC: National Gender and Equality Commission

  • What it does: handles discrimination and equality complaints, especially for women, people with disabilities, children, youth, older people and minority groups.
  • Website: ngeckenya.org
  • Toll free: 0800 720 187
  • Phone: +254 20 20 500 100; WhatsApp +254 748 353 653
  • Email: info@ngeckenya.org
  • Complain online: ngeckenya.org/file-complaint

NLAS: National Legal Aid Service

  • What it does: gives free legal help to people who can’t afford a lawyer. It was set up by the Legal Aid Act, 2016, to make real your right to access to justice (Art 48) and to a lawyer at State expense in serious cases (Art 50(2)(h)).
  • Website: nlas.go.ke (has an “Apply for Legal Aid” button)
  • Toll free: 0800 720 640
  • Email: info@nlas.go.ke

Courts and the law itself

  • The Judiciary: judiciary.go.ke. You can go to the High Court yourself if your rights are violated. No fee may be charged to start that case (Art 22(3)(c)).
  • Kenya Law: kenyalaw.org. The official, free text of the Constitution, every Act of Parliament, and court judgments.
  • Kenya Law Reform Commission: klrc.go.ke. Reviews laws and publishes plain guides.
  • Parliament: parliament.go.ke. Bills, Hansard (the record of what MPs said), and how to petition Parliament (Art 119). Phone (254-2) 2221291 or 2848000.
