# Glossary: the words, in plain language

Words in the order of the alphabet. The Kiswahili word is in brackets where there's a common one.

| Word | What it means | See |
|---|---|---|
| **Act (of Parliament)** | A law passed by Parliament and signed by the President. The Constitution is above every Act. | Art 2 |
| **Advocate** (wakili) | A qualified lawyer allowed to represent you in court. | Lesson 8 |
| **Article** (Ibara) | A numbered section of the Constitution, like "Article 49". Some are split into clauses: 49(1)(f). | |
| **Auditor-General** | The independent officer who checks, after the year ends, whether public money was spent lawfully. Reports go to Parliament and county assemblies. | Art 229 |
| **Bail / bond** (dhamana) | Being let out of custody while your case goes on, on conditions such as paying money or promising to come back. | Art 49(1)(h) |
| **Ballot** (karatasi ya kura) | The paper you mark to vote. In a general election you get six. | Lesson 2 |
| **Bill** (Mswada) | A proposed law, before it is passed and signed. | |
| **Bill of Rights** (Haki za Binadamu) | Chapter Four of the Constitution, Articles 19 to 59. Your rights. | Lesson 1 |
| **By-election** | An election to fill one seat that became empty between general elections. | Art 101(4) |
| **Chapter Six** | The part of the Constitution on **leadership and integrity** (Articles 73 to 80). | Lesson 5 |
| **Code of conduct (electoral)** | IEBC's rules of behaviour that every candidate and party must follow. | Art 84 |
| **Conflict of interest** | When a leader's private interest (business, family) could pull against their public duty. | Art 75 |
| **Consolidated Fund** | The national government's main bank account for public money. | Art 206 |
| **Constituency** (eneo bunge) | The area an MP represents. Kenya has 290. | Art 89, 97 |
| **Controller of Budget** | The independent officer who must approve every withdrawal of public money before it happens. | Art 228 |
| **County** (kaunti) | One of Kenya's 47 devolved units, each with its own government. | Art 6, First Schedule |
| **County assembly** | A county's law-making body, made up of MCAs. | Art 176, 177, 185 |
| **County executive committee** | The Governor, the deputy governor and the county "ministers" (CECs). They run county services. | Art 179, 183 |
| **County Finance Act** | The county law that sets county fees and charges, like market fees and permits. | Art 209 |
| **Devolution** (ugatuzi) | Sharing power and money between the national government and county governments. | Art 174 to 200 |
| **Discrimination** (ubaguzi) | Treating someone worse because of who they are: tribe, sex, religion, disability, age and so on. | Art 27 |
| **Election offence** | A crime connected to an election, such as buying votes or using violence. | Lesson 3 |
| **Election petition** | A court case challenging an election result. | Art 87, 140; Lesson 4 |
| **Electorate** | All the registered voters in an area. | |
| **Equitable share** | The fair share of national money that goes to the counties: at least 15%. | Art 203 |
| **Habeas corpus** | A court order to the people holding someone: bring that person before the court. Can never be limited. | Art 25(d), 51(2) |
| **Hate speech** (matamshi ya chuki) | Words meant to stir up hatred against people because of their ethnicity, race or another ground. Not protected by free speech. | Art 33(2); Lesson 3 |
| **Impeachment** | Removing a President or Governor from office through a vote by elected representatives. | Art 145, 181 |
| **Independent candidate** | A candidate who stands without a political party. | Art 85, 99, 193 |
| **Injunction** | A court order telling someone to stop doing something, or to do something. | Art 23(3) |
| **MCA** | Member of the County Assembly. Represents a ward. | Art 177 |
| **MP** | Member of Parliament. Usually means the member for a constituency in the National Assembly. | Art 97 |
| **National Assembly** | The house of Parliament with 290 constituency MPs, 47 Woman Members and 12 nominated members. | Art 95, 97 |
| **Nominated member** | A member chosen from a party list instead of being directly elected, to represent special groups or balance gender. | Art 97(1)(c), 98, 177 |
| **Petition** (ombi) | A formal written request to a public body, often signed by many people. Also the word for an election court case. | Art 37, 119 |
| **Polling station** (kituo cha kupigia kura) | The place where you vote. Votes are counted there first. | Art 86 |
| **Presiding officer** | The IEBC official in charge of a polling station. Announces the results there. | Art 86(b) |
| **Public participation** (ushirikishaji wa umma) | The public's right to take part in decisions: laws, budgets, fees. | Art 10, 118, 196, 201; Lesson 7 |
| **Recall** | Voters removing their MP or MCA before the end of the term. | Art 104; Lesson 7 |
| **Referendum** (kura ya maoni) | A vote by all citizens on one question, such as changing parts of the Constitution. | Art 255 |
| **Registration centre** | The place where you register as a voter. You may register at only one. | Art 83(2) |
| **Returning officer** | The IEBC official who collates results from many polling stations and announces the result. | Art 86(c) |
| **Senate** | The house of Parliament that protects county interests: 47 elected senators plus nominated members. | Art 96, 98 |
| **Sovereign power** | The highest power in the country. In Kenya it belongs to the people. | Art 1 |
| **State officer** | Someone holding a "State office": the President, MPs, Senators, Governors, MCAs, judges and others. Bound by Chapter Six. | Art 260 |
| **Surcharge** | Making a public officer personally pay back public money they spent unlawfully. | Art 226(5) |
| **Ward** (wadi) | The smallest electoral area. Each elects one MCA. | Art 177(1)(a) |
| **Woman Member** (Mwakilishi wa Wanawake) | The County Woman Member of the National Assembly, elected by the whole county. | Art 97(1)(b) |
