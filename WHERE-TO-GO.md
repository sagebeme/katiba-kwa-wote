# Where to go: who to call, and for what

Every number and address on this page was copied from that office's own website on **Friday, 27 November 2026**.
Numbers change. Before you rely on one, open the website next to it and check.

## Quick list

| I want to... | Go to | How |
|---|---|---|
| Register to vote, check my registration, or find my polling station | **IEBC** | Check online: [verify.iebc.or.ke](https://verify.iebc.or.ke) · Call (020) 2877000 |
| Report someone buying votes, or a candidate using government cars | **IEBC** (election rules) and the **police** (crime) | IEBC: (020) 2877000, info@iebc.or.ke |
| Report hate speech or a leader stirring up hatred between communities | **NCIC** | Toll free **1547** · SMS **1547** |
| Report corruption by a public officer | **EACC** | Toll free **1551** · report@integrity.go.ke |
| Complain about a police officer (beating, shooting, bribe, unlawful arrest) | **IPOA** | Toll free **1559** (24 hours) · complaints@ipoa.go.ke |
| Report a human rights violation | **KNCHR** | SMS **22359** · complaint@knchr.org |
| Complain that a government office ignored me, delayed me or treated me unfairly | **Ombudsman (CAJ)** | Toll free **0800 221 349** · info@ombudsman.go.ke |
| Report discrimination because of gender, disability, age or another reason | **NGEC** | Toll free **0800 720 187** · info@ngeckenya.org |
| Get free legal help because I can't afford a lawyer | **National Legal Aid Service** | Toll free **0800 720 640** · info@nlas.go.ke |
| Read the real words of the Constitution or any law | **Kenya Law** | [kenyalaw.org](https://kenyalaw.org) (free) |

## The offices, one by one

### IEBC: Independent Electoral and Boundaries Commission

- **What it does:** registers voters, keeps the voters' roll, runs elections, registers candidates, enforces the
  electoral code of conduct, and does voter education (Constitution, Art 88(4)).
- **Website:** [iebc.or.ke](https://www.iebc.or.ke)
- **Check your registration:** [verify.iebc.or.ke](https://verify.iebc.or.ke). You type your ID or passport number and your year of birth.
- **Phone:** (254) 020 2877000
- **Email:** info@iebc.or.ke
- **Head office:** Anniversary Towers, 6th Floor, University Way, Nairobi. P.O. Box 45371-00100.
- **Your constituency office:** IEBC has an office in every constituency. The list is on its website:
  [Physical locations of constituency offices (PDF)](https://www.iebc.or.ke/docs/Physical_Locations_of_County_and_Constituency_Offices_in_Kenya.pdf).
- **Complaint forms:** [iebc.or.ke/resources/?Complaint_Forms](https://www.iebc.or.ke/resources/?Complaint_Forms)
- **Note:** we did **not** find an SMS number for checking your registration on IEBC's own website. Some people
  remember one from past elections. Check iebc.or.ke before using any SMS code.

### NCIC: National Cohesion and Integration Commission

- **What it does:** fights hate speech and ethnic hatred, and works for peace between communities. It handles
  complaints under the National Cohesion and Integration Act, 2008.
- **Website:** [cohesion.go.ke](https://cohesion.go.ke)
- **Toll free:** 1547 · **SMS:** 1547
- **Email:** info@cohesion.go.ke
- **Report online:** [cohesion.go.ke/report](https://cohesion.go.ke/report)

### EACC: Ethics and Anti-Corruption Commission

- **What it does:** investigates corruption and enforces Chapter Six (leadership and integrity) of the Constitution
  (Art 79).
- **Website:** [eacc.go.ke](https://eacc.go.ke)
- **Toll free:** 1551
- **Phone:** (020) 4997000 · Mobile 0709 781 000
- **Email:** eacc@integrity.go.ke · Reports: report@integrity.go.ke
- **Report online:** [eacc.go.ke/en/default/report-corruption/](https://eacc.go.ke/en/default/report-corruption/)
- **Head office:** Integrity Centre, Jakaya Kikwete / Valley Road junction, Nairobi. P.O. Box 61130-00200.

### IPOA: Independent Policing Oversight Authority

- **What it does:** receives and investigates complaints against police officers: deaths and injuries caused by
  police, beatings, unlawful arrests, bribes.
- **Website:** [ipoa.go.ke](https://www.ipoa.go.ke)
- **Toll free:** 1559 (the website says it runs 24 hours a day)
- **Phone:** (020) 490 6000
- **Email:** complaints@ipoa.go.ke · info@ipoa.go.ke
- **Post:** P.O. Box 23035-00100, Nairobi

### KNCHR: Kenya National Commission on Human Rights

- **What it does:** receives and investigates complaints that a right in the Bill of Rights has been denied,
  violated or threatened. Every person has the right to complain to it (Art 59(3)).
- **Website:** [knchr.org](https://www.knchr.org)
- **SMS:** 22359
- **Phone:** +254 020 3969000 · Mobile 0726 610 159 · WhatsApp 0798 849 871
- **Email:** complaint@knchr.org (complaints) · haki@knchr.org (general)
- **Report online:** [knchr.org/Report-Violation](https://www.knchr.org/Report-Violation)
- **Head office:** CVS Plaza, 1st Floor, Kasuku Lane, off Lenana Road, Nairobi. P.O. Box 74359-00200.

### CAJ: Commission on Administrative Justice (the Ombudsman)

- **What it does:** handles complaints against government offices and officers: delays, rudeness, unfair treatment,
  abuse of power, and refusal to give you information you have a right to.
- **Website:** [ombudsman.go.ke](https://www.ombudsman.go.ke)
- **Toll free:** 0800 221 349
- **Phone:** 254-20-2270000
- **Email:** info@ombudsman.go.ke
- **Complain online:** [cmis.ombudsman.go.ke](http://cmis.ombudsman.go.ke)
- **Head office:** West End Towers, 2nd Floor, off Waiyaki Way, Westlands, Nairobi. P.O. Box 20414-00200.

### NGEC: National Gender and Equality Commission

- **What it does:** handles discrimination and equality complaints, especially for women, people with disabilities,
  children, youth, older people and minority groups.
- **Website:** [ngeckenya.org](https://www.ngeckenya.org)
- **Toll free:** 0800 720 187
- **Phone:** +254 20 20 500 100 · WhatsApp +254 748 353 653
- **Email:** info@ngeckenya.org
- **Complain online:** [ngeckenya.org/file-complaint](https://www.ngeckenya.org/file-complaint)

### NLAS: National Legal Aid Service

- **What it does:** gives free legal help to people who can't afford a lawyer. It was set up by the Legal Aid Act,
  2016, to make real your right to access to justice (Art 48) and to a lawyer at State expense in serious cases
  (Art 50(2)(h)).
- **Website:** [nlas.go.ke](https://nlas.go.ke) (has an "Apply for Legal Aid" button)
- **Toll free:** 0800 720 640
- **Email:** info@nlas.go.ke

### Courts and the law itself

- **The Judiciary:** [judiciary.go.ke](https://judiciary.go.ke). You can go to the High Court yourself if your rights
  are violated. No fee may be charged to start that case (Art 22(3)(c)).
- **Kenya Law:** [kenyalaw.org](https://kenyalaw.org). The official, free text of the Constitution, every Act of
  Parliament, and court judgments.
- **Kenya Law Reform Commission:** [klrc.go.ke](https://www.klrc.go.ke). Reviews laws and publishes plain guides.
- **Parliament:** [parliament.go.ke](http://www.parliament.go.ke). Bills, Hansard (the record of what MPs said),
  and how to petition Parliament (Art 119). Phone (254-2) 2221291 or 2848000.
