# Your 2027 election checklist

Print this, or screenshot it. Tick each box.

> **Kwa ufupi (Kiswahili):** Jiandikishe, hakikisha maelezo yako, jua kituo chako, mpime mgombea kwa rekodi yake,
> kataa pesa za kura, na ripoti makosa. Uchaguzi mkuu: Jumanne, 10 Agosti 2027.

## Before the registration period closes

- [ ] I am registered as a voter. ([Lesson 2](../lessons/02-registering-and-voting.md))
- [ ] I checked my details at [verify.iebc.or.ke](https://verify.iebc.or.ke): my name, ID number and polling station
      are correct.
- [ ] If I moved, I **transferred** my registration to where I live now.
- [ ] I know the registration dates. (**Check [iebc.or.ke](https://www.iebc.or.ke).** This course doesn't print
      dates it can't confirm.)
- [ ] I helped at least one other person register or check their details.

## Before I choose who to vote for

- [ ] I know what each of the six offices actually does. ([Lesson 6](../lessons/06-who-does-what.md))
- [ ] I asked, or heard answers to, at least one question for each candidate. ([Lesson 5](../lessons/05-leadership-and-integrity.md))
- [ ] I checked whether a candidate has been found to have broken Chapter Six or misused public money.
- [ ] I noticed who was buying votes, and who spoke about other communities with respect.

## During the campaign

- [ ] I refused money for my vote. (Giving **and** taking are crimes: up to KES 2 million, six years, or both.
      [Lesson 3](../lessons/03-election-crimes-and-reporting.md))
- [ ] I didn't forward hate speech. I screenshotted it and reported it to **NCIC (toll free / SMS 1547)**.
- [ ] I reported government vehicles or staff used for campaigning to **IEBC**.

## On election day: Tuesday, 10 August 2027

- [ ] I carried my **original ID or passport**.
- [ ] I know where my polling station is, and how I'll get there.
- [ ] My employer knows I need reasonable time off to vote. (They must allow it, and can't cut my pay: Election
      Offences Act, s.18.)
- [ ] My vote is **secret**. I didn't show or photograph my ballot.
- [ ] I noted the result announced **at my polling station**. ([Lesson 4](../lessons/04-election-disputes.md))

## After the result

- [ ] I waited for IEBC's official declaration, not WhatsApp results.
- [ ] If there's a dispute, I remember it goes to **court**: 7 days to file for the presidency, 28 days for the
      other seats.
- [ ] I kept the peace, and reported anyone stirring up violence to **NCIC (1547)**.
- [ ] I'll keep checking my leaders for five years: budget forums, the county assembly gallery, the Auditor-General's
      report. ([Lesson 7](../lessons/07-public-participation-and-recall.md))
