## Court decisions

| Case | What it decided | Source |
|---|---|---|
| *Katiba Institute & another v Attorney General & another*, Constitutional Petition 209 of 2016, High Court, 14 July 2017 | Declared parts of the recall rules for MPs (Elections Act ss.45, 46, 48) and MCAs (County Governments Act ss.27, 28) unconstitutional | [Kenya Law](https://kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/kehc/2017/4648/eng@2017-07-14) |
| The 2017 presidential election petition, Supreme Court, 1 September 2017 | Declared the presidential election invalid, 4 to 2; a fresh election was held on 26 October 2017 and upheld on 20 November 2017 | [The EastAfrican](https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/news/east-africa/supreme-court-votes-for-democracy-in-kenya-election-petition--1372696); [Jurist, 20 Nov 2017](https://www.jurist.org/news/2017/11/kenya-supreme-court-upholds-presidental-re-election-dnp) |
| Willie Kimani murder trial, High Court, verdict 22 July 2022, sentences 3 February 2023 | Three police officers and an informer convicted; one officer acquitted | [Al Jazeera, 3 Feb 2023](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2023/2/3/kenyan-policemen-sentenced-for-killing-of-human-rights-lawyer); [Nation timeline](https://nation.africa/kenya/news/willie-kimani-murder-a-timeline-3888308) |
| Nyandarua traders' petition, High Court, May 2024 | Status quo order on licence and permit fees while the case was heard. Final outcome **yet to be confirmed** | [The Standard, 30 May 2024](https://standardmedia.co.ke/sports/amp/central/article/2001496205/www.digger.co.ke) |
| MP Onesmus Gikaria's challenge to an IEBC fine, High Court, 8 October 2026 (as reported; we did not read the judgment) | Dismissed the MP's challenge to an IEBC fine of KES 2.5 million over remarks implying voter inducement | [The Standard](https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/national/article/2001552788/iebc-fines-nakuru-town-east-mp-sh25-m-over-voter-inducement-remarks); [Capital FM](https://capitalfm.africa/high-court-clears-way-for-sh2-5m-iebc-fine-against-gikaria/) |

## Where the stories come from

The course is built from the author's own YouTube explainer series on the Constitution (23 scripts, October 2026),
especially Video 01 (*What is a right?*), Video 03 (*The election the court cancelled*), Video 06 (*The lawyer killed
for doing his job*), Video 17 (*The smallest ballot, the biggest bite*) and Video 18 (*The KES 500 vote*), and from
the author's earlier written guide *Kenya's 2010 Constitution in plain words* (Parts 1 and 2). Otieno and Akinyi are
made-up characters from that series. Every fact taken from those materials was re-checked against the sources above.

## What we could not confirm

- **An SMS code for checking voter registration:** we found none on IEBC's website, so the course leaves it out.
- **The police emergency number:** the National Police Service website didn't load (a certificate error), so we left
  the number out.
- **Voter registration dates for 2027:** the IEBC pages we read don't give them. Check iebc.or.ke.
- **Whether any MP or MCA recall has ever been completed:** we found none, so it stays yet to be confirmed.
- **Whether Parliament replaced the MP recall rules struck down in 2017:** we couldn't find that it did. Ask IEBC.
- **The final outcome of the Nyandarua fees case:** we haven't found the final ruling.
